07 / BUSINESS MODEL Growth & Monetization

A lean model that can grow with trust

Start with a campus pilot; test willingness to pay before introducing fees.

POTENTIAL REVENUE

Earn only when value is delivered.

  • 01 Small fee per confirmed booking: Micro-convenience fee only on successful ride activations.
  • 02 Campus / transport partnerships: Preferred transit provider integrations with bus and cab syndicates.
  • 03 Optional premium tools later: Priority notifications and scheduled recurring reservation tools.

NEARBY TOWNS: Expand common return corridors and intercity transit hubs.
3
PARTNERS: Work with trusted institutional campus administrations and verified transport fleets.
